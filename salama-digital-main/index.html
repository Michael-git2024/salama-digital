ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Beyond Reporting

Understanding the drivers of sexual and gender-based violence, barriers to disclosure and help-seeking, and gaps in survivor response systems in Mombasa and Kilifi Counties, Kenya.

Purpose

Generate inclusive, comparative and actionable evidence on forms and drivers of SGBV, disclosure, help-seeking, service utilisation and institutional response gaps.

Research approach

Community surveys, voluntary adult survivor interviews, key-informant interviews, de-identified administrative record review, institutional readiness assessments and referral-pathway analysis.

Salama Digital

A secure, role-based research and evidence-management platform supporting approved instruments, coded records, fieldwork progress, quality assurance and aggregate analytics.

Core principles

Informed consent, survivor-centred practice, inclusion, non-coercion, data minimisation, privacy, accessibility, responsible referral and accountability.

!
Research participant safeguarding

The core research protocol is adult-focused (18+). Direct participation of minors is a separate conditional workstream and requires dedicated protocol, ethics approvals, statutory permissions and specialist safeguarding/referral capacity before recruitment.
