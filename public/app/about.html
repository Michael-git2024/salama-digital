Mombasa & Kilifi SGBV Research Study

Beyond Reporting. Into Understanding.

An evidence-first research initiative examining Sexual and Gender-Based Violence (SGBV) service pathways, institutional readiness, and survivor referral completion across Mombasa and Kilifi counties.

Problem Statement & Study Rationale

Administrative SGBV counts alone rarely capture whether survivors successfully navigate complex referral networks across health facilities, police gender desks, and community shelters. Beyond Reporting pairs structured administrative record abstraction with institutional readiness audits and referral-pathway mapping across urban, informal, and rural sites in Mombasa and Kilifi.

Privacy-First Mandate

Survivor safety and confidentiality govern every layer of Salama Digital. All study records use coded identifiers (e.g., SD-MSA-001), zero direct personal identifiers are stored in analytical views, and access is enforced by strict role-based permissions and audit logging.

Four Priority SGBV Domains

Core thematic areas standardized across field surveys and institutional abstraction.

Intimate Partner Violence (IPV)

Physical, emotional, and economic harm within current or former intimate partnerships.

Sexual Violence

Non-consensual sexual acts, clinical PEP/STI care timeliness, and forensic linkage.

Child / Forced Marriage

Early or non-consensual unions affecting adolescents and coordination with child protection.

Technology-Facilitated GBV (TFGBV)

Online harassment, image-based abuse, and digital coercion across coastal youth networks.
